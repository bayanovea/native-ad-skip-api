# A not_ad on a hidden entry a served one covers leaves the stretch served

An absorbing keep leaves `hidden` untouched, and the chapter check can widen a served entry over a hidden neighbour, so a hidden entry can lie inside a served one. A `not_ad` on that entry's item moves it to `hidden` as `review` and leaves the covering served entry as it is, so viewers still skip that stretch and nothing tells the owner the assessor disagreed.

Product question, nothing to fix until it is answered: should such a verdict reach the owner (or a review item on the covering entry), or narrow the served entry? Counting these verdicts on prod first shows whether it happens at all.
